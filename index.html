Project Overview

BookEase was a web-based appointment scheduling system built on WordPress using Booking Calendar and Contact Form 7, enabling calendar-based booking, automated email notifications, client information collection, and manual payment tracking for small professional practices.

The project ran over 22 weeks with a 20+ member Agile team, consolidating to a core delivery group of 12 over time. I functioned as PMO Coordinator and de facto PMO Lead — owning governance, reporting, RAID management, document control, and meeting discipline end to end. I coordinated nine weekly governance reports, improved on-time submission from ~65% to 95%, managed 20–25 RAID items, reduced overdue actions from five to zero, maintained 12 milestones and 12 deliverables in Microsoft Project and Jira, and supported exception reporting for three approved extensions. Documentation standards I introduced were later adopted as templates for subsequent projects.
